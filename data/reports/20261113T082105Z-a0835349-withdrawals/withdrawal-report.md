# Withdrawn RRF projects: approved budget immediately before withdrawal

**Identified projects: 104. Approved public-investment budget before withdrawal: EUR 2,905,410,704.72.**

Based on the saved Greece 2.0 snapshot through 11 November 2026. No live collection was run.

## Method and interpretation

Sum of approved public-investment (PDE) project budgets immediately before withdrawal, as stated in the approved column of each withdrawal decision; all PDE lines within a project summed once.

Identified withdrawals in the saved catalogue/PDF snapshot. No later same-OPS catalogue record found. Replacement projects or funding transfers are not netted out; nationwide completeness and absence of later reinstatement outside this snapshot are not established.

Public project budgets, including national/VAT components where included by the decision. Not an isolated EU RRF contribution, private co-investment total, contract value, paid amount, or net funding loss.

Projects are counted once by OPS, not by parent action or contract. The 104 projects map to 52 catalogue action codes. All 104 have budget evidence; none is excluded for an unknown amount.

The candidate set combines 103 decisions already classified by the analysis with one additional saved decision (OPS 5203256). Explicit withdrawal language was checked in all 104 operative clauses. The catalogue OPS typo 5079098 was corrected to 5179098 using the decision.
