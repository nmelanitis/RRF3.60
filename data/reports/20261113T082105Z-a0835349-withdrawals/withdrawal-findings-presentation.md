# 2. What ΑΠΕΝΤΑΞΗ establishes

The decisions revoke the projects' inclusion in RRF funding. A withdrawal decision alone does not establish how much work a contractor delivered.

| Question | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Did a project lose its existing RRF inclusion? | Withdrawal or revocation decision |
| Did an individual contract terminate? | Contract cancellation or termination record |
| Did some work reach completion? | Progress and acceptance evidence |
| How much money reached the contractor? | Payment evidence, with settlement confirmation where available |

Projects can have multiple contracts. A withdrawn project may also transfer its scope into a replacement project or another funding programme.

The earlier KIMDIS contract-cancellation figures measure a separate population and cannot be added to this project count.

# 3. Comparison with Greece's RRF allocation

Greece's published RRF allocation is **€35.95 billion**, consisting of **€18.22 billion in grants** and **€17.73 billion in loans**. [Official Greece 2.0 plan](https://greece20.gov.gr/en/the-complete-plan/)

| Comparison | Calculation | Approximate percentage |
|---|---|---:|
| Full RRF allocation | €2,905,410,704.72 ÷ €35,950,000,000 × 100 | **8.08%** |
| Grants component alone | €2,905,410,704.72 ÷ €18,220,000,000 × 100 | **15.95%** |

**These percentages compare scale.** The project-budget numerator includes national contributions and VAT where the decisions include them. The denominators are EU RRF allocations, rounded as published.

Neither comparison measures the share of EU funding lost. Establishing that share would require isolating the RRF contribution and accounting for retained budgets, replacement projects and funding transfers.

# 6. How the count and budget total were calculated

1. Identify withdrawal candidates in the saved catalogue and existing decision classifications.
2. Check explicit withdrawal language in each decision's operative clause.
3. Count each project once by its OPS identifier.
4. Read the **ΕΓΚΕΚΡΙΜΕΝΟΣ ΠΡΟΫΠΟΛΟΓΙΣΜΟΣ ΠΔΕ** column in the withdrawal decision.
5. Sum all relevant PDE budget rows within each project, then sum across the 104 projects.
6. Compare the results with the preceding catalogue history and inspect discrepancies against the saved PDFs.

The approved column records the budget before the withdrawal decision changes it. The proposed column can show a zero or retained budget after withdrawal.

The resulting total covers the approved public-investment budgets in those decisions. It does not include an independently measured payment total or all private co-investment.

# 8. Scope and interpretation

- The count covers **identified withdrawals in the saved snapshot through 11 November 2026**. The source collection had 40 failed PDF downloads, so it cannot establish exhaustive nationwide coverage.
- No later catalogue record for the same corrected OPS was found within that snapshot. Later reinstatements outside the snapshot remain unassessed.
- The gross budget total includes projects whose scope may continue through a replacement project or different funding source. The analysis does not net out those transfers.
- Budget values include national and VAT components where the decisions include them. They do not isolate the EU RRF contribution.
- Withdrawal alone does not prove contract cancellation, absence of delivery or financial loss.

**Suggested wording when presenting the findings:**

> The saved Greece 2.0 records identify 104 withdrawn projects with approved public-investment budgets totalling €2.905 billion immediately before withdrawal. Their gross budget value is equivalent to approximately 8.08% of Greece's published total RRF allocation. This comparison does not establish the amount of EU funding lost.
